Rule number 8: Love before all: If you don't remember any of the rest of these beliefs and tools, remember just this one: Love before all. Start with love, do things with love, end things with love. Before anything have love as the baseline. This is the true encapsulation of being positive sum: to love all before anything else, to love all, to love them infinitely, to care, to truly come towards with a wish to serve, with a wish to give. Loving is all about giving and if nothing else resolves how you should make a decision or do something, then do it with love.
