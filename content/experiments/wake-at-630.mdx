## The test

> I will wake up every day at 6:30 a.m. for the next 15 days or so.

## What it is actually testing

I believe how you live your days is how you live your life. And how you
start your day greatly predetermines how the rest of it is going to be.

Especially when you are betting on yourself and have to rely on your own
agency a lot more. When you've taken the conscious choice to be free from
the rut of the society. There's one more step you have to take, that's not
enough.

Now you have to make sure you work towards a life that you love.

And that starts with a good start to your day.

The goal of this experiment is twofold.

Firstly, when I wake up early, by the time the world is up and running and
communicating, I'm primed and ready to take it on. I have the capacity and
mindspace to do this.

Secondly, I start the day with time to myself. This is important, it
reaffirms to my brain my value in myself. When I wake up early, I naturally
gravitate towards stacking other important activities, such as a workout,
journaling/writing, going out in nature!

Third and the most important.
I strongly believe nothing good happens after 2 AM. And if I take a goal to
wake up at 6:30, eventually 5 am and then maybe 4 o clock too. Then I have
to prep myself to sleep early.

I strongly believe sleeping early, in the right state of mind, preventing
the dread that comes from fatigue, and the bad decisions that follow is
something that can bring about disproportionate compounding positive
change in my life.

Which is the main reason I'm doing this.

To avoid midnight end-of-day fatigue driven dread and bad decisions and
state of mind.

I might have travel and trips in between, but ideally even after getting
back from that, I want to train myself to love and return to this
throughout my life.

## What counts as success

1. I'm on my bed by 10 pm
2. I'm on track to be on bed by 9pm - i.e. detach from phone and screens at
   9pm
3. Eating early to be free by 9pm unless life happens
4. Actually waking up at 6:30
5. Not failing by abandon, I shall track my wake up times. failing at 6:30
   am doesn't mean I wake up then at 10. I can wake up at 7, that's okay.
