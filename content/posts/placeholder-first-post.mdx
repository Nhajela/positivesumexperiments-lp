## What goes here

A post is markdown and nothing else. The record in `src/data/posts.ts` holds
the title, the date and the links; this file holds the writing.

A post can point back into the experiment graph — this one names the
hypothesis _When I am doing well in my life then I will be able to make better
decisions_ and the experiment _Wake up at 6:30 a.m. for 15 days_, which is why
it appears on both of their pages under "Written about this".

## Deleting this

Three files: this one, the record in `src/data/posts.ts`, and the line in
`src/data/bodies.ts`. The build will tell you if you miss one.
