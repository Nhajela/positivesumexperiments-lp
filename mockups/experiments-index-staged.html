The goal of this whole exercise is to frankly make public whatever I try and do, whatever my intents are with anything related to work and beyond work as well.

Two words carry this whole page, so before the lists: what I mean by a hypothesis and an experiment. It’s a small loop, and it looks like this.

The whole system, in one loop.

Hypothesis

A hypothesis is a belief I hold about myself, written down plainly enough that it can turn out to be wrong. Not a goal, not a quote I liked — a bet about how I actually work.

If there’s no way for it to fail, it doesn’t get to be on this page.

Experiment

An experiment is how a belief earns its keep: one concrete change to my days, run for a fixed number of them, with what counts as success written down before I start.

When it ends, the belief comes out stronger, weaker, or gone.
